Two minutes from install to disco

No account, no cloud, no extra hardware. The app talks straight to the hub over your Wi-Fi.

1

Connect the hub

The app finds your DIRIGERA on the network. Press the button on the bottom of the hub once to authorize it.

2

Pick a palette

Rainbow, Sunset, Ocean, Halloween, Christmas and more, or build your own on the color wheel.

3

Press play

Every selected bulb starts cycling in time. Choose Wave, Sync, Random or Strobe and set the tempo.

Built for one thing: the party

The UI stays dark and quiet so all the color lives in the room.

Presets and your own palettes

Ten built-in themes plus an editor: drag the ring, add up to 12 colors, save it with a name.

Four sync modes

Wave ripples through the room, Sync changes every bulb at once, Random surprises, Strobe hits the drop.

Speed, brightness, intensity

From a slow half-minute glow to half-second changes. Dial the saturation back for softer tones.

Keeps going with the phone locked

The loop runs in an Android foreground service with a stop button in the notification shade.

Puts the room back

When the party ends, every bulb returns to the color and brightness it had before.

Local and private

Nothing leaves your Wi-Fi. No analytics, no tracking, no account. Read the privacy policy.

Questions

Does it work with the old TRÅDFRI gateway?

No. The app uses the local API of the IKEA DIRIGERA hub. The square TRÅDFRI gateway is not supported.

Which bulbs can it control?

Any color-capable bulb paired with your DIRIGERA: IKEA TRÅDFRI color bulbs, ORMANÄS LED strips and other color lights that show up in IKEA Home smart. White-spectrum-only bulbs cannot change color, so they are left alone.

Does it need the internet?

No. Your phone and the hub only need to be on the same Wi-Fi. There is no cloud account and no server of ours in between.

Does it keep running when I lock the phone?

Yes. The color loop runs in a native Android foreground service, so it survives a locked screen and app switching. A notification shows a stop button.

Why are two lights free?

Two lights are enough to experience everything, including the Wave and Sync modes. Pro is a one-time 39 kr that funds development. There is no subscription and no ads.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. Disco for Dirigera is Android-only today.

Is this an IKEA app?

No. It is an independent project by a Swedish developer. "DIRIGERA", "TRÅDFRI" and "IKEA" are trademarks of Inter IKEA Systems B.V. The app is not endorsed by or affiliated with IKEA.
